# CaseZero Film Design System

## Overview

CaseZero uses a restrained editorial banking language: a pale paper field, very dark ink, hairline rules, large tightly set headlines and small monospaced evidence labels. The live page is spacious and asymmetric, with the customer story on the left and the working product on the right. Product views feel like formal records rather than glossy SaaS cards. The marketing film may add cinematic human context, but every product moment must preserve this precise visual character.

## Colors

- **Paper**: `#E4EAE5` — primary light product surface.
- **Panel**: `#F2F5F2` — inset forms and evidence panels.
- **Ink**: `#131A15` — primary text and structural lines.
- **Negative**: `#0B0F0C` — cinematic background and inverted product bands.
- **Secondary Ink**: `#5A665D` — supporting copy.
- **Guilloche**: `#A8BFB0` — fine-line decorative security pattern.
- **Endorsement**: `#8B2333` — the only strong accent; calls, stamps and progress.
- **Focus**: `#365F48` — verified or positive state.

## Typography

- **Instrument Sans** (`400–700`, variable) — customer language and major product statements. Use weight 620 for hero-scale copy and 400 for supporting copy.
- **Martian Mono SemiExpanded** (`100–800`, variable) — evidence labels, URLs, timestamps and small operational language. Use wide tracking and uppercase sparingly.
- **Hierarchy** — film headlines 72–116px, support copy 24–34px, evidence labels 18–22px. Never use web-sized body copy in the rendered film.

## Elevation

Depth comes from 1px ink or sage rules, offset paper layers and real camera depth. Product frames may use a quiet 24–60px shadow when placed into a cinematic environment, but the interface itself stays flat. Avoid glossy glass cards, neon bloom and generic SaaS gradients.

## Components

- **Complaint Composer** — a bordered RFC-style form that preserves the visitor's words and evidence.
- **Boardroom Weave** — six specialist seats arranged around a fine-line oval with a small decision card at the centre.
- **Evidence Minutes** — a vertical, clickable record of each handoff and its supporting receipt.
- **Human Review Register** — one case, one evidence summary and three clear human decisions.
- **Mission Control** — a full operational view of deadlines, workload, case movement and review pressure.
- **Endorsement Stamp** — burgundy proof mark used only when a result is genuinely verified.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do's

- Preserve actual CaseZero screenshots and recorded UI; never redraw the product.
- Use negative space, fine rules and one dominant message per scene.
- Let real-world scenes carry warmth while product scenes remain precise.
- Use slow focus pulls, controlled dolly moves and restrained editorial cuts.
- Keep burgundy rare so it reads as a decision or endorsement.

### Don'ts

- Do not use avatars, presenters, talking heads or people speaking to camera.
- Do not use feature grids, slide layouts, bullet walls or architecture diagrams.
- Do not use holograms, robots, glowing AI brains or generic 3D icons.
- Do not fabricate product UI or place invented metrics inside CaseZero screens.
- Do not use bright gradients, pill-heavy layouts, emojis or playful bounce motion.
