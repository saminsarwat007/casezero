# SCRIPT — CaseZero product film

**Voice:** Hope (HeyGen)
**Voice settings:** natural pace · calm confidence · restrained emphasis
**Voice direction:** Warm, precise, and conversational. Say “Case Zero” as two words. No sales-announcer energy and no technical tone.

---

## Line 1 — The complaint vanishes (Frame 1)

**Time:** 0.0 – 9.0s
**Delivery:** Quiet recognition; let “disappears” land.

    A bank complaint can feel like it disappears the moment you send it—leaving uncertainty, unanswered updates, and no clear path forward.

## Line 2 — Your words, your evidence (Frame 2)

**Time:** 9.0 – 18.0s
**Delivery:** Turn from uncertainty to agency.

    Case Zero changes that. Write what happened in your own words, then attach the evidence you already have.

## Line 3 — The case moves (Frame 3)

**Time:** 18.0 – 31.0s
**Delivery:** Measured and reassuring; one clean beat per sentence.

    From there, the right information comes together. Records are checked. The deadline is watched. And the next step is prepared.

## Line 4 — Judgment stays human (Frame 4)

**Time:** 31.0 – 44.0s
**Delivery:** Firm, human, and transparent.

    When a case needs judgment, Case Zero stops for a real person. Technology should support that decision, not hide it. Nothing important is hidden.

## Line 5 — One clear outcome (Frame 5)

**Time:** 44.0 – 56.0s
**Delivery:** Build confidence, then invite action without hype.

    Teams spend less time chasing systems. Every decision can be seen and explained. One complaint in. One clear outcome out. Try Case Zero with your own complaint. Scan to get started.
