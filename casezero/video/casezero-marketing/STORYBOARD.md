## Video direction

Product-only visual system. Use the real CaseZero captures without redrawing, restyling, or inventing interface states. Deep forest carries the opening and close; soft bone carries product proof; burgundy appears only on the decisive words and the human-review boundary. Instrument Sans is the display voice and Martian Mono is quiet editorial chrome. Motion uses long-tail `power3` settles and purposeful camera reframes; each product detail appears only when the narration names it. All scene joins are clean editorial hard cuts — no generated transitions. Frame 4 is the deliberate breather: the review screen resolves and holds so the human-in-the-loop message can be read. No humans, avatars, stock footage, browser chrome, fake cursors, fake UI, feature grids, holograms, gradients, emojis, bounce, lazy breathing, back-half drifting, slideshow front-loading, or screensaver motion. Keep important content above the bottom caption band.
