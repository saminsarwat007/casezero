## Intent

Create a premium CaseZero SaaS marketing film that can open or close a stakeholder presentation. It must tell a human story in everyday language: a customer submits a real complaint in their own words, sees the case move, and knows a real person intervenes whenever judgment matters.

## Customizations

- Use real CaseZero pixels for every product shot; never fabricate or restyle the interface.
- Use product-only cinematic crops and restrained brand motion for the full story.
- Keep on-screen copy sparse and understandable in one glance.
- End with the live URL and QR code.

## Notes

- No humans of any kind, no avatars, no presenter, no talking head, no stock footage, no emojis, no fake holograms, no robots, no feature grids, no slide layouts, and no technical architecture language.
- One consistent CaseZero palette: deep forest, soft bone, muted sage, and one burgundy accent.
- Calm, assured, high-class commercial tone; not a tutorial and not a presentation deck.
