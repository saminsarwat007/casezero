Six specialists / one rulebook

Each seat has a job—and a boundary.

Seat 01 / Intake

Intake

Structures the email and PDF, then encrypts personal data.

Stops unsafe instructions before any model reads them.
Seat 02 / Classifier

Classifier

Identifies the complaint type, urgency and confidence.

Low confidence cannot quietly become a decision.
Seat 03 / Verifier

Verifier

Checks CRM and core banking records against the claim.

Compares evidence. It never invents bank facts.
Seat 04 / Resolver

Resolver

Drafts the remedy when the evidence is complete.

Cannot post without PASS evidence and a signed ticket.
Seat 05 / Communicator

Communicator

Writes a clear customer update in the bank's voice.

Required regulatory wording comes from policy, not prose.
Seat 06 / Supervisor

Supervisor

Watches the deadline and the load across the team.

Escalates exceptions; it does not hide them.
